# Personal Website — Design Spec

## Goal
Position Savinodh Shetty as a credible bridge between engineering and product/project leadership —
someone who has shipped real things and is now stepping into managing them.

---

## Audience
- Hiring managers & recruiters for PM / TPM roles
- Engineering leads evaluating technical credibility
- Collaborators or network connections doing a quick background check

---

## Core Message (above the fold)
> "I build things. Now I help teams build better things."

One-liner that signals the transition without disowning the engineering identity.

---

## Page Structure

### 1. Hero
- Name + title: `Engineer → Product/Project Manager`
- One-sentence positioning statement
- CTA buttons: **View Resume** | **Get in Touch**

### 2. About
- 3–4 sentences: engineering background → what drew you to PM → what you bring
- Brief narrative, not a bio wall of text

### 3. Skills (dual-column)

| Engineering (Foundation)        | PM / Leadership (Direction)       |
|---------------------------------|-----------------------------------|
| System design, code, infra      | Roadmapping, prioritization       |
| Debugging, performance tuning   | Stakeholder communication         |
| CI/CD, DevOps                   | Agile / Scrum, JIRA, Confluence   |

### 4. Featured Projects
3–4 cards, each with:
- Title + one-line description
- Your role: `Built` / `Led` / `Managed`
- Key outcome / impact (e.g. "shipped X, reduced Y by Z%")
- Optional link to repo, case study, or demo

### 5. Experience Timeline
Compact — company, role, date, one-bullet highlight per role.
Emphasize scope growth: IC → tech lead → PM-adjacent.

### 6. Contact / CTA
- Email link
- LinkedIn
- GitHub (signals ongoing hands-on credibility)
- Download resume button

---

## Visual Direction

- **Layout:** Single-page scroll with smooth section anchors
- **Palette:** White/off-white base, single accent color (navy `#1E3A5F` or slate `#475569`)
- **Typography:** Inter or DM Sans — clean, modern, readable
- **Spacing:** Generous whitespace, no clutter
- **Mobile-first:** Fully responsive, looks great on phone and desktop

---

## Tone
Professional but human. Confident without being boastful.
Write for a PM hiring manager who may not be deeply technical — avoid engineering jargon.

---

## Tech Stack
- **Framework:** Next.js (App Router)
- **Styling:** Tailwind CSS
- **Deployment:** Vercel
- **Content:** Hardcoded in JSX/JSON — no CMS needed for v1

---

## Out of Scope (v1)
- Blog or articles section
- Dark mode toggle
- Heavy animations (subtle fade-ins only)
- Multi-language support
